Four routes, countless discoveries.

Day hikes, treks and tailor-made itineraries across Corsica — from the mountains of Central Corsica to the coastal valleys.

Based in Biguglia (near Bastia), I guide international travellers across Corsica: from easy coastal walks to demanding mountain routes. Each outing is adapted to your level, season and weather, with a strong focus on safety and local knowledge.

Popular areas: Central Mountains, Balagne, Cap Corse, Restonica, and tailor-made itineraries on request.

01 / Guided experience

Biguglia Evening Hike

Evening hike Easy → moderate May to October

A relaxed evening hike starting from the village of Biguglia, former medieval capital of Corsica. This gentle heritage walk combines late-day mountain light, peaceful trails and panoramic views, ending with a tasting of local Corsican products.

View details
Start time: 6 pm, from May to October.
Meeting point: village of Biguglia, near Bastia.
Focus: heritage discovery, nature, viewpoints, evening atmosphere.
Route: village walk and castle ruins, hike to a Romanesque chapel, aperitif with local products, return with head torch.
Level: easy – suitable for beginners and families.
Elevation gain: approx. 200 m.
Duration: 2.5 to 4 hours depending on pace.
Group size: small groups for comfort and safety.
Equipment: hiking shoes, water, warm layer; head torch provided if needed.

02 / Guided experience

Paglia Orba

2 days Challenging June → September

A legendary Corsican summit for hikers seeking elevation gain and spectacular scenery. A demanding but carefully guided itinerary, with strong focus on safety, pacing and mountain awareness.

View details
Season: June to September.
Format: 2-day trek for fit hikers, with an overnight stay in the refuge or in a tent nearby, depending on availability and arrangements.
Refuge: Ciottulu di i Mori Refuge (highest refuge in Corsica).
Day 1: approach hike (~700 m ascent), possible swim, arrival at the refuge early afternoon.
Optional: evening viewpoint walk around the refuge (weather and group condition permitting).
Day 2: ascent of Paglia Orba (technical sections, 2–2.5 h climb) + ~600 m ascent, then descent via the refuge.
Level: challenging – excellent physical condition required.
Equipment: hiking boots, water, windproof jacket, warm layer, sleeping bag (overnight stay at around 2,000 m, in the refuge or in a tent nearby, depending on availability and arrangements).
Good to know: tents with ground mats provided (no need to bring one).

03 / Guided experience

Corte → Ota Traverse Trek

Trek Intermediate → challenging Tailor-made

A great multi-day traverse to discover the heart of Corsica, from the central mountains to valleys opening towards the coast. A demanding yet accessible trek for fit hikers, fully tailor-made depending on the number of days, pace and mountain conditions.

View details
Format: multi-day traverse (customisable).
Season: June to September.
Terrain: mountain trails, possible off-GR variants depending on conditions.
Level: intermediate to challenging depending on the chosen version.
Organisation: stages, breaks, safety management, weather adaptation.
Accommodation: refuges, guesthouses or bivouac depending on the route.
On request: number of days, logistics, carrying, guiding.

04 / Guided experience

Nordic Walking – Introduction Session

Technique Beginner friendly Well-being

A 1.5 to 2-hour introduction to Nordic walking: learning the basics (posture, pole use, rhythm) to walk more efficiently, longer and engage the whole body without strain. Accessible to everyone — ideal for getting back into activity or complementing hiking.

View details
Season: October to May (mainly for locals).
Duration: 1.5 to 2 hours.
Level: very easy – no previous experience required.
Goals: walking technique, gentle cardio, full-body strengthening, well-being.
Equipment: comfortable clothing, trainers, water (poles available if needed).
Audience: locals, beginners, fitness restart, recreational athletes.
