01 / The spirit

Follow the season.
Take your time.

Winter reveals another Corsica. Quieter trails, villages with stories to tell, landscapes seen in a different light.

Over five days, I guide you between nature and culture: hikes, snowshoeing when conditions allow, villages, heritage, history and gastronomy.

From November to early April, the programme remains flexible. It evolves with the weather, ground conditions, your interests and your level of experience. Hiking, snowshoeing when conditions allow, villages and heritage visits can be combined to suit each day.

06 / Good to know

Before you travel.

Is there a fixed day-by-day itinerary?

We agree on your main interests together. The order and content of each day evolve with the weather, access and ground conditions.

Is snowshoeing guaranteed?

No. Snowshoeing is an option when snow and conditions allow. Other discoveries may take its place.

What fitness level is needed?

The required level will be discussed before booking, based on the planned outings and your experience.

Who books accommodation and transport?

You book your accommodation and transport separately. They are not included in the advertised guiding price.

When can I come and where do we start?

From November to early April, with dates arranged on request, subject to availability. Starting points and areas explored are flexible, chosen to suit your group and the programme.

Can I enquire on my own or as a pair?

Yes. Solo travellers and pairs are welcome to enquire and receive a personalised quote. The advertised price of €500 per person for five guided days applies to groups of at least three participants.
